## What it covers

1. **What it is and who it is for.** WorkBud, an OJT hours and expense tracker
   for students on placement, shown on the deployed site.
2. **The main flow.** Sign in, log a day with its expenses, watch the dashboard
   update, then export the time log.
3. **The code.** The three pieces and how they talk: the React client in
   `client/`, the Express API in `server/`, and the Supabase database.
4. **How AI was used.** What the assistant drafted, what I wrote myself, and
   where it got things wrong. The written record is [AI-USAGE.md](../AI-USAGE.md).
5. **What I would do next.** The same list as the known issues in the main
   [README](../README.md).
